## 2. The project in one paragraph

Love in Pixels is a private, browser-based, pastel pixel-art puzzle adventure meant as a personal gift. The player picks one of five characters, picks a story mode, and explores a connected 2D world solving puzzles and collecting rewards (golden hearts, letters, photos, music, video). Original plan: six locations, starting with The Little Town, built as a hybrid of exploration and puzzle rooms.

## 3. Decisions made in this conversation

1. **Five playable characters** replace the single customizable protagonist from the original document.
2. **Three story modes:** Adventure, Romance and Horror. Only Adventure has a story so far.
3. **Landscape only.** Portrait shows a "Turn your device sideways" screen. On phones the game also asks for full screen and a landscape lock (some browsers refuse).
4. **Touch only.** No keyboard controls anywhere (menus or game). Movement is an on-screen pad, there is a Jump button, and interaction is tapping the "!" that appears over something nearby.
5. **Smooth movement.** Acceleration and deceleration, equal diagonal speed, sliding along walls, 3x render resolution so there are no pixel jumps, small walking bounce.
6. **Difficulty:** not very challenging but not very easy (medium).
7. **The Keeper is a separate character from Kofi.**
8. Horror should be spooky and atmospheric, not gory (my assumption, not yet confirmed).

## 5. Story: Adventure mode, "The Scattered Memories" (chosen)

**Premise.** The Little Somewhere is held together by the **Memory Lantern** at the top of the Memory Castle. It shatters into six **Memory Stars**. A pale fog called **the Hush** drains colour and makes people forget. The explorer (the player's character) follows notes from the mysterious **Keeper**.

**Cast.**
- **Lumen, the Keeper:** warm, slightly absent-minded old keeper of the Lantern. Signs notes "The Keeper". His name is revealed in Chapter 5.
- **Kofi:** hooded messenger who delivers the Keeper's notes and never stays to explain. His identity is his own mystery.
- Gospel, Sergeant Owusu-Brown, Kobby and Missy are allies in the world (see the table above for replacements).

**Chapters.**
1. **The Little Town:** Gospel's torn picture; Missing Pieces puzzle; flower gate. Reward: first golden hearts, a letter.
2. **Garden of Beginnings:** flower sequence, butterfly trail. First Memory Star, a photograph.
3. **The Little Café:** forgotten order, secret table, conversation puzzle. Second Star, a music piece. Kobby hides a Star fragment out of fear of losing it.
4. **Bridge of Stars:** constellation, river crossing, light sequence. Third Star, a personal video message. The Sergeant guards the bridge.
5. **Memory Castle:** archive, locked library, final door using clues from every chapter. Fourth Star and the biggest reveal (the Keeper's name and secret).
6. **Place of a Thousand Stars:** finale combining every puzzle type. Relight the Lantern; the creator's final personal surprise.

**Twist.** The Keeper created the Hush by accident: he hid the world's memories to protect them, but hidden memories fade. The ending is not a fight; the explorer helps him choose to remember. Kofi's mystery: if the player picks Kofi, he is the messenger himself and has forgotten why he carries the notes. For any other player, the final Memory Star returns Kofi's name.

**Tone.** Cozy and hopeful. Humour from Gospel's questions, the Sergeant's mission briefings and Kobby's deadpan.

### Alternative story (not chosen): "The Skyfall Expedition"

A Skystone crashes into the town fountain and opens a hidden stairway. A sarcastic talking Wandering Map guides the explorer in a race to carry the stone to the Place of a Thousand Stars before the new moon. Kobby is a friendly rival; each chapter has a surprise (an upside-down First Town, a café that exists only at night, a betrayal that is actually a test, an upside-down castle). Twist: the Skystone is the explorer's own forgotten memory. Two endings plus a secret ending and random events for replay. Keep as raw material if we want more surprises.

### 6.3 The Missing Pieces puzzle (designed, not built)

Stage 1, find four pieces:

| Piece | Where | Type | Solution | Gentle hint |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Town fountain | Pattern | Press the three spouts in the order the water jumps: left, right, middle | Gospel: "Watch the water." |
| 2 | Mrs. Adjoa's shop | Hidden object | Find her glasses in the flower bed, return them | "Somewhere pretty, with flowers." |
| 3 | Chest under the bench | Combination lock | Three dials set to the house door colours from the east side: pink, mint, blue | "Read them from the sunrise side." |
| 4 | Hedge maze at the edge of town | Maze 7×7 | Walk to the centre; one dead end, no failure | Gospel shouts left or right at forks |

Stage 2, assemble:
1. Drag the four pieces into the frame (matching coloured edges; wrong piece bounces back).
2. Picture splits into 9 tiles with one gap; slide them back. Shuffle by 20 random legal moves so it is always solvable. No move limit.
3. Picture glows, Keeper's note appears, brass key slides out.

**Hints (three levels, no penalty):** gentle (nudge toward the area), stronger (name the object), solution help (show the answer). Available after 30 seconds stuck.
**Reward:** first golden hearts, first personal letter, brass key to the Garden of Beginnings.

| Element | Setting |
| --- | --- |

| Chest lock | 3 dials, colour clues visible in town |

Later chapters raise difficulty through complexity, not obscurity (for example a 4×4 board in the Café).

## 7. What is built

The playable file is a single HTML page (Appendix A).

**Flow:** character select → story mode select → game (Adventure only).

**Character select:** five pixel sprites drawn in code, names only on the cards, details panel (role, blurb, draft perk). Selection is remembered in `localStorage` (`lip_character`, `lip_mode`).

**Story mode select:** Adventure (key icon), Romance (heart), Horror (ghost). Selecting Horror turns the screen dark purple with a red accent. Romance and Horror currently lead to a placeholder welcome screen.

**Chapter 1 playable skeleton (Adventure):**
- 20×12 tile town (16 px tiles) with trees, two houses, fountain, paths, flower beds and a flower gate on the east edge.
- Player: on-screen pad movement with easing, Jump button (a visual hop only; it does not clear obstacles), tap the "!" to interact.
- Scene 1 note shows at the start. Gospel (or Bea) gives Scene 2. Four picture pieces and three golden hearts are plain pickups. Collecting all four triggers Scene 5 and the brass key. The gate runs Scene 6 and ends Chapter 1.
- Dialogue uses the player's character lines from 6.2.
- HUD: hearts, pieces, key, current objective, Menu button.

**Not built yet:**
- The five mini-puzzles (fountain, glasses, chest, maze, sliding board).
- Mrs. Adjoa and her shop; the benches and chest.
- Saving progress, letters/photo/music/video rewards, inventory and journal screens.
- Sound and music, real sprite art, tilemap from Tiled.
- Chapters 2 to 6, Romance mode, Horror mode, character perks.

**Known limitations:** untested on a real phone (button sizes, speed and tap accuracy may need tuning); graphics are code-drawn placeholders; the landscape lock only works where the browser allows it.

## 8. Technical notes for resuming

- **Logical size:** 320×192 (20×12 tiles of 16 px), rendered to a 960×576 canvas (`SC=3`) and scaled by CSS with `image-rendering: pixelated`.
- **Movement:** `SPD=64` px/s, exponential smoothing on velocity, per-axis collision against solid tiles `T H F G`. Jump uses `GRAV=520`, `JV=135`.
- **Entities (`ENTS`):** `note`, `gospel`, `gate`, `p1`–`p4` (pieces), `h1`–`h3` (hearts), each with a tile position and type. `near()` finds the closest entity within 22 px; a tap within 26 px of it calls `act(entity)`.
- **Dialogue:** `say([[speaker, text], ...], callback)`; tapping the box or canvas advances it.
- **Character data (`CHARS`)** holds colours and proportions for the sprite generator `draw()`. Per-character lines live in `REACT`, `PIECE`, `WHOLE`, `GATE6`.
- **Publishing:** the page is a hosted artifact; small changes were made with targeted text edits. The source in Appendix A can be republished as a fresh artifact if the link is lost.
- **Original architecture plan:** Phaser 3, Tiled, Aseprite, JSON data files, LocalStorage saves (see the original document). The prototype is plain HTML and canvas; migrating is a deliberate later decision.
